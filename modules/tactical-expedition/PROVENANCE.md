# Provenance: tactical-expedition

This campaign is original Rusty Tales content. The gallery, encounter
rosters, companion, text, map and event chains were written for the tactical
combat authoring example and copy no published setting, monster, or adventure
text. Its creatures and policies come from the original `tactical-bestiaire`
extension.

The campaign requires the open-licensed `three-action` ruleset for its
three-action tactical economy, character creation and actions, and the original
`placeholder-art` assets module for logical presentation references. Those
dependencies carry their own licence texts and notices. This campaign adds no
licensed rules text and no media files of its own.
