# Provenance: sample-crypt

All content in this module (areas, events, variables and the campaign definition) is original, written for Rusty Goldbox as a small sample campaign. It uses the classic ruleset's monsters and items and the placeholder-art assets by their logical IDs.

The gate shrine and its five-gold healing fee are original sample content.
Its heal operation uses the required ruleset's track and the existing
operation vocabulary.
