# Campaign NPC authoring notes

`wylda_figwort` is the optional pre-Brugh companion: a level-2 fifth-srd
Rogue with the ordinary `criminal` and `alert` choices from the published
ruleset and the existing `blackapple-art:wylda_portrait` asset. The source
identifies Wylda as 19; fifth-srd character files only accept an age alongside
a lifepath ledger, so the unsupported age field is omitted rather than making
the template invalid.

`master_ned` is the optional post-rescue companion: a brown talking war
horse from C26. The extension's `talking_warhorse` race and
`warsteed_companion` creation/class data give him three levels, 18 hit points,
60-foot speed and a natural Hooves action. His equipment is empty; anatomy is
not a transferable item. Recruitment respects the campaign's six-member limit,
and Ned remains separate from the seven child records.

Master Ned uses the independently approved original
`blackapple-art:master_ned_portrait` portrait, showing his brown horse form.
The dungeon's glamour image depicts the source's white-unicorn illusion;
it does not replace his character identity.
