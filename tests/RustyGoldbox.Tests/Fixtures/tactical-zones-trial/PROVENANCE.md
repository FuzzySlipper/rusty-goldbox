# Shared-zone training hall

This original integration fixture uses the original `ascend` and
`tactical-zones` test rulesets. Its room, events and text copy no published
setting, rules text or tables. Figure definitions reference the repository's
placeholder assets through their owning module.

The campaign is independently packable and makes the shared-zone,
three-action combat shape reachable through the ordinary title, party and
menu controls.

The training striker gives members with the extension's original Brace guard
feat a visible optional hit reaction. Its fixed damage supports comparing
acceptance and decline after loading the same pending-decision save.
