# Provenance: jrpg-art

Original 16-bit SNES/PS1-style pixel art created for the Rusty Tales **Crystal
Keep** classic JRPG showcase (generated locally on Strix Halo via Z-Image Turbo,
seed `1993`, then flat-field de-vignetted, period-snapped for seamless tiling,
cut out cleanly against flat slate ground, and packed as 8-bit RGBA PNGs).

| Asset | File | Dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `keep_wall` | `walls/keep.png` | 512 × 128 (`wall`, `door`, `floor`, `ceiling`, 128 × 128 each) |
| `blade_portrait`, `whitemage_portrait`, `blackmage_portrait`, `ranger_portrait` | `portraits/*.png` | 96 × 96 portraits |
| `hero_blade`, `hero_whitemage`, `hero_blackmage`, `hero_ranger` | `sprites/hero_*.png` | 4-frame animated side-view hero sheets (64 × 96 frames) |
| `monster_slime`, `monster_golem`, `monster_drake` | `sprites/monster_*.png` | 4-frame animated side-view monster sheets (`64×64`, `80×104`, `96×112` frames) |
| `gem_icon` | `icons/gem.png` | 32 × 32 encounter icon |
| `citadel` | `backdrops/citadel.png` | 320 × 200 twilight crystal citadel battle backdrop |
| `crystal_shrine` | `pictures/crystal_shrine.png` | 1280 × 200 animated 4-frame guardian crystal (320 × 200 frames, 4 fps) |
| `crystal_*` | `skins/crystal_*.png` | Royal-blue & silver classic JRPG window skin (`crystal`) |
