# Provenance: wizardry-art

Original 1990s dark-fantasy pen-and-ink and gouache artwork created for the
Rusty Tales **Wizardry Delve** first-person dungeon crawler showcase (generated
locally on Strix Halo via Krea 2 Turbo with `civitai_retro_dark_fantasy_krea2`
at strength `0.8`, seed `1993`, then flat-field de-vignetted, period-snapped
for seamless tiling, cut out cleanly against flat slate ground, and packed as
8-bit RGBA PNGs).

| Asset | File | Dimensions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `catacomb_wall` | `walls/catacomb.png` | 512 × 128 (`wall`, `door`, `floor`, `ceiling`, 128 × 128 each) |
| `knight_portrait`, `priest_portrait`, `mage_portrait`, `rogue_portrait` | `portraits/*.png` | 96 × 96 portraits |
| `bone_guard`, `gargoyle`, `cultist`, `dread_knight` | `sprites/*.png` | 4-frame animated front-facing monster sheets (96 × 112 frames) |
| `skull_icon` | `icons/skull.png` | 32 × 32 encounter icon |
| `sanctum` | `backdrops/sanctum.png` | 320 × 200 gothic battle backdrop |
| `shrine` | `pictures/shrine.png` | 1280 × 200 animated 4-frame oracle shrine (320 × 200 frames, 4 fps) |
| `grimoire_*` | `skins/grimoire_*.png` | Gothic iron & candlelit parchment UI skin (`grimoire`) |
