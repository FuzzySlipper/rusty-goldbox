## Simplifications

- Attributes are kept as scores (modifier = (score − 10) / 2), so a boost adds
  2 and the partial boost at +4 or more adds 1 — the same modifiers the
  Remaster rules give.
- Flanking is approximated: a melee attacker with an ally adjacent to the
  target treats it as off-guard, without checking opposite sides. Initiative
  doesn't move when a character is knocked out. Range increments have no
  penalty; diagonals cost one square. Armor Strength, check penalties and
  Speed penalties aren't modelled.
- Shield Block answers a confirmed physical hit, reduces the pending damage by
  the shield's Hardness, and applies the remaining damage to the shield's
  material hit points and to the creature. Reactive Strike answers only a
  creature leaving reach.
- The wolf's Knockdown follows a hitting Strike as one two-action use; Pack
  Attack counts allies adjacent to the target. The zombie's Grab and bite,
  resistances and immunities, the boar's Ferocity and Charge, and the bandit's
  ambush and Dread Striker aren't modelled. Giant centipede venom is one
  Fortitude save on a hit (1d4 on a failure, 2d4 on a critical failure)
  instead of an affliction with stages.
- Heal uses its two-action range; its three-action area and its harm to undead
  aren't used. Bless covers the whole party for 10 rounds. Spell heightening
  applies to cantrips and divine font heals only. The wizard has no school
  slot or arcane bond; there are no Treat Wounds, skills or items beyond
  gear.
- Experience assumes a level 1 party: each character gets the whole award.
