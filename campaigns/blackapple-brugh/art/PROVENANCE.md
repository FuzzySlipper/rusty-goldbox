# Blackapple Brugh art provenance

## Scope

This record covers the original reference images and art-direction material
under `campaigns/blackapple-brugh/art/`. It accompanies the campaign's
[root provenance record](../PROVENANCE.md); it does not relicense the
`fifth-srd` ruleset or Engine code.

## Source relationship and exclusion

The adapted campaign is based on *The Blackapple Brugh*, 1st Edition Release
21, by Kyle Hettinger, copyright 2020–2021 and 2023. The reviewed source is
`KH1-The-Blackapple-Brugh-r21.pdf`, SHA-256
`87e22e0d0bbdb66c54010d4485df05e92d0237b0234c2dffa24298250d4f6de5`. Its
textual materials, maps, floorplans, diagrams, charts, and forms are covered
by the source's CC BY-SA 4.0 notice. The source's other artwork is used with
permission and is excluded from redistribution.

The generated images in this directory use only written adaptation anchors
and original text prompts. No source PDF page, map image, cover, or excluded
third-party illustration was supplied as a generation or edit input. The
images are new illustrated interpretations of the campaign's village, court,
characters, and paired perception concept; they are not claims that the
source's credited artists contributed to this adaptation.

## Generated-art status

Each accepted image is marked in [REFERENCE_MANIFEST.md](REFERENCE_MANIFEST.md)
with its logical asset ID, prompt file, tool path, source-input status, output
checksum, dimensions, alpha status, and inspection decision. The generation
tool is the built-in Codex `image_gen` path. When the tool does not expose a
model or sampler setting, the manifest records that fact instead of guessing.

The project intent is to distribute these original adaptation images with the
Blackapple campaign under CC BY-SA 4.0, alongside the complete licence and
adaptation/source notices already retained at the campaign root. The later
asset integrator must carry this record, the root attribution, and the prompt
metadata into any exported `blackapple-art` module. Do not export the supplied
source PDF or any excluded source artwork.

The final Wylda and Ibix references are bounded judge-directed edits of their
preserved original generated references. Wylda was reframed for a central
square crop; Ibix was proportioned as a compact child-shaped-at-distance fae
double. Their local pre-correction inputs, first rejected edit outputs, exact
second prompts, built-in output IDs, checksums, and display/original inspection
decisions are retained in [REFERENCE_MANIFEST.md](REFERENCE_MANIFEST.md).
Those edits used only workspace-generated references as inputs and did not use
any source PDF or excluded illustration.
